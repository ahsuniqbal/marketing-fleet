# Voice guide

**Stage:** F.2b · **Agent:** brand-strategist
**Reads:** `foundation/brand-strategy.md`, `foundation/social-research.md`,
`foundation/intake.md`, any copy the client already likes
**Writes:** `clients/<client>/foundation/voice-guide.md` (template:
`templates/client-voice-guide.md`)

`copywriter` writes from this, `video-producer` picks voiceover from this, and `brand-reviewer`
fails deliverables against it. Adjectives like "friendly but professional" cannot be enforced;
examples can.

## Procedure

1. **Three to four voice traits.** Each as "X, not Y" — "confident, not arrogant" — with one
   sentence on what it means in practice.
2. **Do / don't pairs.** For each trait, two rewritten lines: the off-voice version and the
   on-voice version of the same message.
3. **Tone by context.** How the voice flexes per platform in scope (TikTok vs LinkedIn), per
   funnel stage (awareness vs retargeting), and per situation (launch, apology, offer).
4. **Example lines — at least ten.** Hooks, headlines, CTAs, and a short caption, written in the
   voice and tied to the messaging pillars. These are the reference set.
5. **Vocabulary.**
   - Words we use (including how the customer names the problem, from research)
   - Banned words — clichés, competitor terms, and anything the claims policy forbids
   - Spelling and naming: product names, capitalization, regional spelling
6. **Formatting rules.** Emoji (which, how many, where), hashtags (count, branded tags), case
   in headlines, numerals vs words, punctuation habits, CTA wording.
7. **Language.** If more than one working language is in `CONVENTIONS.md`, state per language
   whether to write natively or translate, and code-mixing rules (e.g. English with Urdu).
8. **Voiceover direction.** Pace, energy, gender/age range if the client has a preference,
   accent — `video-producer` uses this to pick an ElevenLabs or Kokoro voice.

## Checklist

- [ ] Traits written as "X, not Y"
- [ ] Every trait has do/don't rewrites
- [ ] Tone shifts per platform in scope and per funnel stage
- [ ] At least ten example lines tied to pillars
- [ ] Banned words include claims-policy terms
- [ ] Emoji, hashtag, and CTA rules explicit
- [ ] Language and code-mixing rules stated
- [ ] Voiceover direction written

## Failure modes

- **Adjectives only.** Nobody can review against "warm and approachable".
- **One tone for every platform.** LinkedIn copy on TikTok dies.
- **No banned list.** Every campaign rediscovers the same cliché.
- **Examples off-pillar.** Example lines that do not carry the strategy teach the wrong thing.

## Handoff

🚦 Client approves positioning and voice. Then `brand-kit` at F.3a.
